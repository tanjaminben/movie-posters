runtime dependencies. The surrounding portfolio supplies its own font files,

approval is not evidence of permission from a film-rights owner. Undocumented
third-party permissions remain unknown. This record makes no legal determination
or new commercial print/distribution claim.

rating fetch, IMDb logo, or affiliation claim. `src/posters-data.json` retains the

Historical production helpers and image-generation receipts are not included.
