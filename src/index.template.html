BEHIND THE COLLECTION

Why I make these posters

I watch an absurd amount of horror movies. I'd probably have no problem recognizing 99% of the movie posters you put in front of me.

I was inspired to make something fun, memorable, and recognizable for privacy.

I want colleagues to feel comfortable bringing the serious stuff to me, too. The posters are my way of saying, “We can make this work. We can make privacy fun. Come talk to me.”

With Halloween approaching, the timing felt right. I paired familiar movie posters with privacy wordplay: a small change to the title, a twist on the tagline, a joke that fit the theme.

Some people recognized the film but wanted a little more explanation of the privacy reference (see: Drag Me to Sell).

For me, if a poster sparks a conversation, it’s doing its job.
